Label Sheet Format

Each A4 page contains a 3×5 grid of QR code labels. Each label includes:

  • The asset's unique QR code (encodes the live asset URL)
  • The asset tag (e.g. CSE-042) printed below the QR
  • The asset name truncated to fit the label

Print on A4 label sheets or plain paper, then cut and stick to the physical asset.
